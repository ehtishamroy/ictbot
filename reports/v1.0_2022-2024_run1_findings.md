## A real bug found and fixed
The first run produced **0 trades**. Instrumented diagnostics traced it to the
engine re-evaluating DEF-DOL-01 (draw-on-liquidity) **every 5-minute bar** using
the *current* price, instead of once at the killzone open as the spec's A2
section literally states ("all three [narrative conditions] must be TRUE at
08:30 NY"). This mattered because DOL's reach is a distance from current price
to the *opposite* pool — and at the exact bar a sweep fires, price is by
definition at one extreme of the day's range, i.e. structurally farthest from
that opposite pool. Re-checking DOL at the sweep bar rejected genuine setups
almost by construction.

**Fix**: the narrative gate (bias + DOL + shallow-floor) is now evaluated once,
at each trading day's first killzone bar, and cached for the day
(`engine/state_machine.py`). The LTF mechanical trigger (sweep → MSS → FVG)
still runs reactively, bar by bar, as before. All 60 existing tests still pass
unchanged (the hand-built fixtures use constant bias/PDH/PDL across all bars, so
freezing at killzone-open didn't move their expected outcomes).

## Why: a structural (not buggy) tension in the fixed constants
- `dol_reach_atr_1h = 3.0` (fixed, non-free per A6) → typical reach ≈ 44-46 pips
  (3× ATR(1H,14), mean ATR(1H) ≈ 14.6 pips over this window).
- Actual EURUSD PDH-PDL day range, 2022-2024: **median 66.7 pips**, mean 72.7,
  IQR 46-95 pips.
- Because the range is usually wider than the reach threshold, a price close
  enough to the DOL target to pass N2 is usually too far from the opposite pool
  to still need (or be able to complete within a 2.5h window) a full sweep of
  it. This is a geometric property of *this exact combination* of a fixed
  reach constant and real EURUSD volatility — it is not addressable by any of
  the seven free parameters: `mss_window`, `fvg_min`, `expiry_bars`,
  `disp_mult`, `sweep_close_n`, `sl_buffer_points` all sit **downstream** of
  this bottleneck; none of them touch DOL eligibility or the sweep-vs-DOL
  distance relationship, and `dol_reach_atr_1h` itself is explicitly listed as
  a **fixed constant, not optimized** in the spec (A6).
